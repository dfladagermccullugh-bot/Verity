Before you start

You will talk to an automated AI interviewer, not a person. It asks a short survey about public libraries, word for word, and then shows how it read each answer. It takes about three minutes. You can stop at any time.

If you use your voice, your browser turns your speech into text; in Chrome that means your audio is sent to Google. This demo stores nothing you say: when you close the page it is gone.
