Verity Voice demo

An AI interviewer asks a short survey about public libraries, word for word, and shows how it read each answer.

Before you start

You will talk to an automated AI interviewer, not a person. It takes about three minutes. You can stop at any time.

If you use your voice, your browser turns your speech into text; in Chrome that means your audio is sent to Google. This demo stores nothing you say: when you close the page it is gone.

The keypad works like a phone's: press the number of an option, or a number followed by #. Say "stop" to end, or "remove me" to see how opting out works.
